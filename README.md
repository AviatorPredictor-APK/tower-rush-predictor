## 🏗️ What Is the Tower Rush Game?

Tower Rush is a crash-style casino game built around a tower that grows one floor at a time. A player sets a stake, adds another floor and then chooses whether to continue or collect the current value. A failed placement ends the round and can remove the active stake.

Unlike a curve-based crash game, the decision is repeated after each successful floor. The higher the tower becomes, the more valuable the potential payout may look—and the more the player risks by continuing.

## ✖️ Floors, Multipliers and Cashout

The active **Tower Rush multiplier** can change as the tower grows. The predictor divides a round into three invented signal zones:

- 🟢 **Floors 1–5** → “safe zone,” early cashout targets
- 🟡 **Floors 6–10** → “balanced zone,” medium targets
- 🔴 **Floor 11+** → “rush zone,” high volatility and aggressive targets

A cashout locks the displayed result only if it completes before a failed floor. No stop point guarantees profit, and a higher multiplier does not make the next build safer.

## 🧭 Tower Rush Strategy and Bankroll Limits
Basic risk limits include:

1. Set a maximum loss before the first round.
2. Keep the stake fixed instead of increasing it after a collapse.
3. Choose a cashout target in advance and do not chase losses.
4. Treat every stake as money that can be lost in full.

These are harm-limitation rules, not a winning system.
